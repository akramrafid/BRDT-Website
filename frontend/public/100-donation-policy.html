100% Donation Policy

Your trust is our greatest strength

Why We Say We Have a 100% Donation Policy

At Belghar Rural Development Trust, our promise is beautifully simple and deeply sincere: Every penny you give goes straight to those who need it most. Not a single penny is spent on admin, salaries, or office costs.

This is our 100% Donation Commitment — a trust we hold close to our hearts and honour in every project, every decision, every act of service.

How We Cover Our Administrative Costs

To protect this precious model, all administrative and operational expenses are lovingly covered through:

  • BRDT Trustees & Board Members
  • Monthly contributions from BRDT general members

This means your donations remain pure, untouched, and used only for helping people in need.

Transparency You Can Trust

For over six years, donors from around the world have placed their trust in us — and we cherish that trust deeply. Through experience, dedication, and heartfelt commitment, we ensure your donation reaches the right hands quickly, effectively, and with complete transparency.

No one is more devoted to the honest and impactful distribution of your donations than BRDT. We understand the great Amanah (trust) you give us, and we carry it with humility and responsibility.

A Responsibility We Hold with Care

The responsibility upon us is great, and we feel it every day. We pour our time, energy, and sincere effort into researching, planning, and delivering the most effective routes for distributing your donations.

Our vision remains unwavering: To provide a robust, transparent, and deeply compassionate distribution service for every donor who chooses to make a difference through us.
