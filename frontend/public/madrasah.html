Our Institution

A Vital Role in Shaping the Future

Belghar Rural Development Trust is already playing a vital role in shaping the future of underprivileged children by renovating and running Belghar Madinatul Ulum Madrasa and Orphanage. About 113 students are studying in this Madrasa. Among them, 81 students are enrolled in the Nurani section, and 33 students are studying Hifz and Nazera in the Orphan Boarding School, Through this institution, the Trust provides a modern, age-appropriate, safe, caring and well-organized environment where children receive both academic and moral education.

The Madrasa imparts quality education rooted in cultural and spiritual values, which will ensure that students grow up with strong character, discipline and a sense of purpose.

The Orphanage

Compassion & Holistic Development

The orphanage, run with the same compassionate approach, provides shelter, food, healthcare and psychological support to children who have lost their parents’ affection. The Trust ensures that every child is treated with dignity and is given the opportunity to learn, grow and dream.

Beyond basic needs, the organization focuses on holistic development - encouraging participation in extra-curricular activities, sports, travel, competitive skill-building programs and social engagement.

  • Shelter & Food
  • Healthcare Needs
  • Psychological Support
  • Extra-curriculars

Currently, the cost of running a madrasa boarding school for Hafez and orphans is about seven to eight lakh taka per year. There is also the salary of the teachers. We want Hafez and orphans to grow up eating good quality food in this boarding school. We will arrange good teachers for them, and we will pay good salaries to respected teachers. You can give money for the education of the madrasa students, you can support the boarding of the madrasa by giving two or four bags of rice, lentils, beef or goat for food. In this way, you too will be entitled to the reward of the work of Sadaqah-e-Jariyah and we will also be free from various pressures.

"Belghar Rural Development Trust, through its dedication and integrity in running Belghar Madinatul Ulum Madrasa and Orphanage, continues to transform the lives of children."

Its efforts not only lead to the personal development of children, but also strengthen the social fabric of the entire upazila and install hope and opportunity where it is needed most.

Sponsor a Hafiz

Supporting Our Hafiz Students

Sponsor a ‘Hafiz’ Student

Empower a vulnerable child to learn and memorise the Holy Qur’an through our 3-Year Hifz Sponsorship Programme.

"The best among you are those who learn the Qur’an and teach it." — Bukhari, Abu Dawud: 1452

A Path to Lifelong Blessings

Our Hifz Sponsorship Programme is dedicated to making Islamic education and Qur’an memorisation accessible to vulnerable children, including orphans. Through this programme, children are given the opportunity to engage deeply with their faith, develop strong Islamic character, and grow into pious, well-rounded individuals.

Your sponsorship becomes a source of Sadaqah Jariyah—a continuous charity that benefits you long after you are gone.

🌟 Donate Now — Change a Life Forever

For Tk. 20,000 (£125/$165) per year, or Tk. 60,000 (£370/$495) for the full three years, you can sponsor a Hafiz student.

If you wish, you may also contribute Tk. 2,000 (£13/$17) per year towards the child’s clothing.

Your sponsorship helps provide:

  • Qur’anic education
  • Hifz memorisation support
  • Food and safe accommodation
  • Healthcare and essential daily needs
  • A nurturing environment for spiritual and personal growth

Support an Orphaned Child

By sponsoring an orphan through their Islamic education, you become part of a beautiful journey. Your support ensures they receive the guidance, care, and resources needed to memorise the Holy Qur’an and build a brighter future.

Your generosity today can transform a child’s tomorrow.

The Reward of Sponsoring a Hafiz

One of the most spiritually rewarding acts of Sadaqah Jariyah you can undertake.

Preserve Allah's Word

Every Hafiz who memorises the Quran is a living guardian of Allah's word. By sponsoring their journey, you become an instrument of Quran preservation — earning reward every time the student recites, teaches, or leads prayers for the rest of their life.

Intercession on Qiyamah

The Prophet ﷺ taught that on the Day of Judgement, the Hafiz will be dressed in a crown of honour and their parents will be given a light that outshines the sun. As a sponsor, you share in supporting this noble journey and its eternal rewards.

Raise Islamic Scholars

Today's Hafiz students become tomorrow's Imams, teachers, and Islamic scholars. Your sponsorship doesn't just help one student — it ripples through generations as they go on to teach others, lead communities, and inspire future Huffaz across Bangladesh.
